# REQ-001 — Claims extraction for older population

## Business request

Provide healthcare claims recorded in 2025
for synthetic patients aged 65 years or older.

## Population

Patients aged 65+ in 2025.

## Period

2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31.

## Required variables

- claim_id
- patient_id
- sex
- region
- procedure_date
- procedure_code
- procedure_label
- reimbursement_amount
- patient_co_payment

## Output

CSV controlled extract.

## Data source

Validated claims only.

## Status

Approved for synthetic demonstration.
